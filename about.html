Eggs with a story

Sebbielog is a salted egg business based in Santa Cruz, Laguna. We offer salted egg and fresh duck egg options in different sizes and conditions for customers to choose from.

The people behind Sebbielog

Locally owned and grown

The factory is owned by Justine Patinio and Abhie Santos. Their business began in 2019 and serves customers looking for egg products in Santa Cruz, Laguna.
